CIVICPULSE SLA AND ESCALATION POLICY

Document Type:
Project Demonstration Policy

Purpose:
This document defines indicative service-level targets and escalation procedures for municipal complaints.

SLA TARGETS:

Pothole:
HIGH priority — 48 hours

Road Damage:
MEDIUM priority — 72 hours

Water Leakage:
HIGH priority — 12 hours

No Water Supply:
HIGH priority — 12 hours

Garbage Not Collected:
MEDIUM priority — 24 hours

Overflowing Garbage Bin:
MEDIUM priority — 24 hours

Illegal Dumping:
HIGH priority — 24 hours

Streetlight Not Working:
MEDIUM priority — 48 hours

Drainage Blockage:
HIGH priority — 24 hours

Sewage Overflow:
CRITICAL priority — 4 hours

Public Health:
HIGH priority — 24 hours

Public Safety:
CRITICAL priority — 4 hours

Park Maintenance:
LOW priority — 72 hours

Broken Footpath:
MEDIUM priority — 72 hours

Damaged Road Sign:
MEDIUM priority — 48 hours

ESCALATION LEVELS:

Level 0:
Automated assignment and monitoring.

Level 1:
Department-level officer escalation when the SLA is approaching or an operational issue occurs.

Level 2:
Ward-level escalation when the complaint is overdue or remains unresolved.

Level 3:
Zone-level escalation for continued SLA breaches.

Level 4:
Municipal authority escalation for critical or prolonged unresolved complaints.

Critical public safety complaints should receive immediate attention.

The SLA deadline is calculated from the complaint creation time and the applicable SLA rule.

The deterministic SLA configuration in the database is the system of record for deadline calculation. The RAG agent should not override database SLA values.
