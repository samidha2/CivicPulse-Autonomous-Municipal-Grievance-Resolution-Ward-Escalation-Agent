CIVICPULSE MUNICIPAL COMPLAINT HANDLING POLICY

Document Type:
Project Demonstration Policy

Purpose:
This policy defines the standard process for receiving, understanding, categorizing, routing, monitoring, resolving and closing municipal citizen complaints.

Complaint Lifecycle:

1. SUBMITTED
A complaint is submitted by a citizen through the CivicPulse portal or chatbot.

2. UNDER_REVIEW
The complaint is being analyzed by the AI system or municipal staff.

3. ASSIGNED
The complaint has been assigned to the appropriate municipal department and officer.

4. IN_PROGRESS
The assigned officer or work team has started working on the complaint.

5. PENDING_INFORMATION
Additional information is required from the citizen or another municipal department.

6. RESOLVED
The assigned team has completed the corrective action.

7. CITIZEN_CONFIRMED
The citizen has confirmed that the issue has been resolved.

8. CITIZEN_REJECTED
The citizen does not agree that the issue has been resolved.

9. REOPENED
A previously resolved complaint has been reopened because the issue remains unresolved.

10. CLOSED
The complaint has been successfully completed and closed.

AI Processing:
The AI system may classify complaints, identify possible duplicates, recommend departments, determine severity and retrieve relevant policy information.

Human Oversight:
AI recommendations involving critical public safety matters, uncertain classification or conflicting information should be reviewed by an authorized municipal officer.

Duplicate Complaints:
When multiple citizens report the same physical issue, the system may link the newer complaint to an existing complaint rather than creating unnecessary duplicate work orders.
