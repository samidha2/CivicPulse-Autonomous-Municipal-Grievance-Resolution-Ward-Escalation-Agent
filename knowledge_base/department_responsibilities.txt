CIVICPULSE DEPARTMENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Document Type:
Project Demonstration Policy

ROADS AND INFRASTRUCTURE:
Responsible for potholes, damaged roads, broken footpaths and damaged road signs.

WATER SUPPLY:
Responsible for water leakage, no water supply and water distribution-related complaints.

SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT:
Responsible for garbage not collected, overflowing garbage bins and illegal dumping.

STREET LIGHTING:
Responsible for streetlights that are damaged or not functioning.

DRAINAGE AND SEWERAGE:
Responsible for drainage blockage and sewage overflow.

PUBLIC HEALTH:
Responsible for complaints involving sanitation, public health hazards and health-related municipal concerns.

PARKS AND PUBLIC SPACES:
Responsible for park maintenance and public-space maintenance.

TRAFFIC AND PUBLIC SAFETY:
Responsible for public safety incidents and traffic-related municipal safety concerns.

Routing:
The AI system should recommend a department based on the complaint category and description.

Department assignment should be validated against the configured department-category mapping before creating operational work orders.
