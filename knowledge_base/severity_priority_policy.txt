CIVICPULSE SEVERITY AND PRIORITY POLICY

Document Type:
Project Demonstration Policy

Severity indicates the potential impact and urgency of a complaint.

LOW:
Limited impact with minimal immediate risk.

MEDIUM:
Moderate impact affecting citizens or municipal services.

HIGH:
Significant impact affecting multiple citizens, essential services or public infrastructure.

CRITICAL:
Immediate risk to public safety, health or essential services.

Priority Guidelines:

LOW priority:
Routine maintenance and low-impact issues.

MEDIUM priority:
Operational issues requiring timely municipal action.

HIGH priority:
Issues affecting essential services, significant infrastructure or multiple citizens.

CRITICAL priority:
Public safety emergencies, sewage overflow and situations involving immediate health or safety risks.

Examples:

A small park maintenance issue may be LOW.

A broken footpath may be MEDIUM.

A major water leakage affecting residents may be HIGH.

A sewage overflow creating an immediate health hazard may be CRITICAL.

A public safety complaint involving immediate danger should be treated as CRITICAL.

AI-generated severity and priority are recommendations and should be validated against configured municipal rules.
