CIVICPULSE CITIZEN COMMUNICATION POLICY

Document Type:
Project Demonstration Policy

Purpose:
Citizens should receive clear and understandable information about the progress of their complaints.

Complaint Submission:
After successful submission, the citizen should receive the complaint number and acknowledgement.

Assignment:
The citizen may be informed when the complaint has been assigned to the responsible department.

Work Progress:
The citizen should receive relevant status updates when the complaint moves into active resolution.

Resolution:
When a complaint is marked resolved, the citizen should receive a resolution notification.

Feedback:
Citizens should be given an opportunity to confirm whether the issue has actually been resolved.

Rejected Resolution:
If the citizen rejects the resolution, the complaint may be reopened for further investigation.

Duplicate Complaint:
If a complaint is identified as a potential duplicate, the citizen should receive an explanation that the issue has been linked to an existing complaint where appropriate.

Communication should avoid exposing unnecessary internal information, personal data or confidential operational details.
